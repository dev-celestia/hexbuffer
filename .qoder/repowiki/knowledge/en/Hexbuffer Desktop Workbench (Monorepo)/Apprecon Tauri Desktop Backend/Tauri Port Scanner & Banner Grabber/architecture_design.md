The module is a Tauri plugin organized as a Rust crate under `src-tauri/src/port-scanner/`. `mod.rs` declares the six submodules (`banner`, `scanner`, `services`, `state`, `targets`, `types`) and exposes two `#[tauri::command]` entry points: `scan_ports` (async) and `stop_port_scan` (sync). `PortScanState` is a Tauri-managed global state holding an `Arc<Mutex<HashMap<String, Arc<AtomicBool>>>>` of active scan cancellations keyed by `scan_id`. The scanning pipeline flows through layered modules: `targets.rs` normalizes hosts and expands IPv4 CIDR ranges; `scanner.rs` performs the actual `tokio::net::TcpStream::connect` with timeout and delegates to `banner.rs` for protocol-specific banner probing (HTTP(S), SMTP, POP3, IMAP); `services.rs` maps ports and banner content to service names. Concurrency is controlled via a `tokio::Semaphore` bounded by the request's `concurrency` parameter, and results are emitted to the frontend through `app.emit` on per-scan channels (`port-scan-result-{id}`, `port-scan-progress-{id}`). Results are also forwarded to `crate::automation::ingest_port_scan_result` for downstream processing. Dependency direction is strictly inward: `mod.rs` → `scanner` → `banner`/`services`; `types.rs` is consumed everywhere but defines no dependencies.
